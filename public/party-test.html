Frunk
Watch Party car test

Checks whether this car can run a Watch Party. Do tests 1–3 parked, then 4–6 with a second car or a phone on the call.

Sign in to Frunk first, then come back to this page. Open Frunk

1

Microphone

Tap Allow, accept the car's prompt, then say something.

2

Voice call

Start a call here and enter the code on the other car or phone (open there). Passes once you hear each other.

or

3

Video

Plays a sample stream (the same format Plex sends). Passes after 20 seconds of smooth playback.

4

Rear screen while driving

With the call and video running: on the rear screen tap Join Front Display, then shift into Drive and go for a short drive. Does the video keep playing and can the rear seat still hear and be heard?

5

Echo

With the video playing out loud and the call on, stay quiet and ask the other person: can they hear the movie through the call?

6

Voice from another tab

With the call on, open Disney+ in a new tab and keep talking for a minute, then come back to this tab. Could you still hear each other?

Results go to the Frunk server log, so you can read them back later.
